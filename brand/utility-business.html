02 · Product roadmap

Three ideas to build Utility Business

We start by building useful software, attract users by solving real problems, and develop sustainable ways to support and grow the platform.

IDEA 01 · FIRST PRIORITY

Business software toolkit

Build practical web applications for everyday business tasks: invoice and quotation generators, expense and profit trackers, business document creators, and project planning tools. Start with a small number of excellent tools, then expand based on user needs.

IDEA 02 · EXPANSION

Business productivity platform

Bring multiple tools together in one accessible platform for freelancers, entrepreneurs and small teams. Over time, explore accounts, saved projects, premium features and subscription plans where they genuinely provide additional value.

IDEA 03 · MONETIZATION

Advertising and sustainable growth

Explore Google AdSense on eligible, useful website content, such as tutorials and business guides. Keep advertising separate from software controls and results. Consider premium features and other appropriate revenue streams as the platform grows.

✓

Our principle: value comes first

Utility Business is a software project first and an advertising opportunity second. We aim to create tools people genuinely want to use. AdSense requires website approval and policy compliance; revenue is not guaranteed, and advertising eligibility for website content does not automatically extend to a browser extension.
