04 · Growth strategy

Build value first.
Grow with purpose.

Useful tools and genuinely helpful content can form the foundation of a sustainable website.

IDEA 01

Free online tools

Offer useful calculators, text formatters, converters and productivity helpers. Provide clear instructions and original examples for each tool.

IDEA 02

Helpful tutorials

Publish original guides that solve specific problems. Include practical steps, real examples and relevant answers to common questions.

IDEA 03

Curated tool collections

Group related tools into useful topic pages for writing, calculations, conversions and productivity. Make every page valuable on its own.

Advertising with responsibility.

Google AdSense approval and earnings are not guaranteed. Build original, useful content and review Google's current programme policies before applying. Never click your own ads or encourage invalid clicks. Keep advertisements clearly distinguishable from navigation, tool controls and results. Do not assume a website advertising approval covers a browser extension or another product interface.
