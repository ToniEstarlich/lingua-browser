Logo & Design Notes
The visual identity

Language,
made simpler.

Utility Lingua brings language assistance into a simple, recognizable symbol. The logo combines a bold L-shaped form with two dots, set against a vivid orange background.

LINGUA · PRIMARY ICON
Design commentary

What makes the logo work?

01 / THE L

A clear identity

The L is the central shape of the mark. Its geometric, continuous form gives the logo a compact silhouette that can be recognized at a glance.

02 / THE TWO DOTS

Two points of connection

The two dots add a distinctive visual signature. They can suggest conversation, exchange, or two connected points in a language interaction.

03 / THE COLOURS

Energy and clarity

Bright orange makes the icon stand out, while dark charcoal gives the symbol strong contrast and keeps the design restrained.

Brand relationship

One utility brand. A distinct language product.

Utility uses its signature lime green, #DFFF45. Lingua uses orange, #FF9500, to establish its own identity within the wider Utility family. The shared minimalist approach connects the products, while the colour makes Lingua easy to distinguish.

Small-size use

Designed for the browser toolbar

A browser extension icon must remain recognizable at small sizes. The design therefore uses only two dots, one simple L-shaped stroke, and two solid colours. Avoid adding tiny text or unnecessary decorative details to the icon itself.
